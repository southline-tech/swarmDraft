Made for long-form writing

Chapters, outline and review stay together while AI editors leave suggestions you can keep or skip.

  • Suggestions, never rewrites

    Every AI edit shows up as a tracked suggestion. Accept what helps, reject what doesn’t — your manuscript only changes when you say so.

  • Your book stays on your computer

    Saves as you go; the original Word file is never changed. Your chapters and settings stay on your PC, not in someone else’s cloud.

  • Bring your own AI editor

    Connect editor bots safely; each gets its own key, nothing leaves your machine. You choose which tools work on your book.

  • Free and open source

    Download, inspect, and improve the app on GitHub. Installers ship from Releases; this site links there instead of hosting large files.
