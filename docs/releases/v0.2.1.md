## What's new in 0.2.1 (pilot QA)

This update fixes problems found during the first full pilot on Windows after upgrading from 0.1.1
to 0.2.0. Your writing stays on this computer; nothing here turns on cloud saving or sync.

### Saving and your book file

- **Autosave after an upgrade** — Books that still pointed at old app folders are moved into your
  current swarmDraft data folder so saves work again.
- **Save a copy** — The save dialog now starts in your **Documents** folder, not hidden app
  folders. After you save a Word copy, you can **save your book there** to keep working and clear
  the warning banner.
- **Save As** — Choose where your `.draft` book file lives (Documents by default, with your title
  prefilled). **Save** (Ctrl+S) then writes to that file.

### Opening the app

- **No more empty flash** — While swarmDraft checks for your last book, you see “Opening your
  book…” instead of the import welcome screen.
- **Pick up where you left off** — When you reopen a book, you return to the same chapter and
  scroll position.

### Settings and menu polish

- **Settings** sits above review and comment panels so nothing covers your preferences.
- The book menu label **Import Word .docx** fits cleanly with its shortcut, with a simple document
  icon (not a trademarked product logo).

---

**Updating from 0.2.0:** Use **Check for updates** in the book menu, or install the latest build
from GitHub Releases. Your manuscripts and settings are kept.
